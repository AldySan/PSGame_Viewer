# Code of Conduct

## Why this exists

PSGame-Viewer is a hobby project, and the community around it should feel the same way: a place where anyone can ask a question, report a broken file or propose a change without worrying about how they will be treated. This page describes what that means in practice. It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Our expectations

**Be respectful.** People join with different levels of experience. A question that looks basic to you may be someone's first day with Linux or with game files. Answer it the way you'd like your own first question answered.

**Assume good intent.** A terse bug report usually means someone was in a hurry, not that they don't care. Ask for the missing details instead of criticizing what's there.

**Keep it constructive.** Critique ideas and code, not people. "This path breaks on read-only folders" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.

**Stay on topic.** Keep threads about the project. Political debates, self-promotion and unrelated support requests belong elsewhere.

**Respect licensing.** Do not upload copyrighted game files, keys or dumps. Use synthetic samples when reporting parser issues — the test fixtures show how.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory comments about anyone or their background
- Publishing other people's private information
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, trolling or baiting
- Sharing copyrighted or illegal content

## If something happens

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (profile: AldySan). Reports are handled confidentially and taken seriously.

Consequences depend on the situation: a warning, deletion of the content, or a ban from the project spaces for repeated or severe cases.

## A closing note

Most of this comes down to common sense: be the person you'd want to meet in an issue thread. If anything here is unclear, just ask — the maintainer doesn't bite.
